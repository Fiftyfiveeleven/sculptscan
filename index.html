Getting a good scan

  • Stand 2.5–3 m (8–10 ft) from the camera, with the camera at hip height and level.
  • Wear fitted clothing, tie long hair up, and use a plain background with light in front of you.
  • Turn slowly through a full circle with your arms about 45° from your sides.
  • Scan in the same place, at the same time of day, for fair before/after comparisons.

Precision calibration

In the scanner choose print the precision marker. Print it at 100% / Actual size, measure the 10 cm check line, tape the page flat on your stomach, then choose Calibrate first → Calibrate with marker.

Subscriptions

  • SculptScan Pro unlocks body scans, before/after comparisons, the live progress overlay and scan reminders.
  • Gym plans (Starter, Pro, Unlimited) turn a Mac into a member kiosk.
  • Manage or cancel any subscription in the Mac App Store: your name → Account Settings → Subscriptions. Use Restore purchases in the app after reinstalling.

Gym mode

  • Staff open the admin area with the Staff button and the admin PIN.
  • Forgotten member PIN: Staff → Members → select the member → Reset PIN.
  • Member data lives only on the gym's Mac — back it up with Time Machine.
