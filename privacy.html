SculptScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

SculptScan is a body-scan and health-planning app for Mac and iPhone, published by Fifty Five 11 ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your data stays on your device. We don't run servers that receive it, we don't use analytics or advertising, and we never see your photos, measurements or health information.

Information the app stores on your device

  • Profile and plans — name (optional), age, sex, height, weight, goals, food preferences, training preferences and the workout, nutrition and habit plans generated from them.
  • Body scans — camera photos of each side of your body taken during a scan, your body outline, estimated measurements and scores.
  • Logs — workouts, meals, habits, weight, sleep, mood and other entries you make.
  • Settings — such as units, calibration results and reminder preferences.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your Mac or iPhone. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else (except progress you choose to share with your gym, described below). You can export a backup file yourself from the Profile page, and delete everything with Profile → Delete all data.

Camera

The camera is used only while you are scanning or calibrating. Body detection runs entirely on your device using an on-device model bundled with the app. Camera images are never transmitted.

Gym mode

When a gym uses SculptScan on a shared Mac, each member's information is stored in a separate, private area on that Mac, protected by the member's PIN. Gym staff can manage member accounts (name, member number, optional email) but cannot see a member's scans, photos or logs unless that member chooses to share:

  • Share my progress shows staff a summary: body score, scan dates, workouts completed and weight change.
  • Also share my latest scan photo additionally shows staff the front photo from the latest scan.

Members can turn sharing off at any time, which deletes what was shared. When staff delete a member, that member's data is permanently removed from the Mac. The gym is responsible for the security of its Mac and for any progress reports it exports.

Gym members on iPhone

A gym can give a member a personal code that unlocks SculptScan Pro on the member's own iPhone. To check the code, the app reads a small membership record that the gym's Mac stores in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit): the gym's name, whether the membership is active, on hold or cancelled, and when the gym's plan renews. It contains no member name, contact details or health information. All of the member's own data stays on their iPhone.

If the member turns on Share my progress, the iPhone uploads the same summary described above (and, if chosen, the latest front scan photo) to iCloud so the gym's Mac can show it. This summary is encrypted on the iPhone with a key derived from the member's code, so only someone with that code — the gym — can read it; Apple and we cannot. Sharing requires the member to be signed in to iCloud. Turning sharing off, leaving the gym, or the gym cancelling the code deletes the shared copy. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you turn on scan reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device. No information is sent anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No accounts or sign-in with us.

Health information

SculptScan provides estimates from a 2D camera image and general fitness guidance. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or nutrition program.

Children

SculptScan is not directed to children under 13 and the app's profile setup requires an age of 13 or over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
